Opportunity Solution Tree

Start from one outcome, list the customer opportunities that could move it, the solutions for each, and the experiments that would test them.

    Reading the tree

    Outcome: a measurable change in customer behaviour. Opportunity: a need, pain or desire heard from customers, not a feature. Solution: something you could build. Experiment: the cheapest test of the riskiest assumption behind a solution. Compare opportunities with each other before you compare solutions.
